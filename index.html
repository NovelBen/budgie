Google Sheets Auto-Sync

Expenses auto-append to your personal sheet in the background.

Paste the URL from your deployed Google Apps Script.
Step-by-step: How to setup & update Google Sheet script ▾
  1. Open your spreadsheet in Google Sheets ("Budgie Expenses").
  2. In Google Sheets menu, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any code, click Copy Apps Script Code below, and paste it into the editor.
  4. In top right, click Deploy → Manage deployments (or New deployment).
  5. Click the Pencil icon (Edit) → under Version select New version → click Deploy.
  6. Copy the Web App URL ending in /exec and paste it above!
